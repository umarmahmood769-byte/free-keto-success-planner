    What These Numbers Actually Mean

    BMI

    A general screening measure based on your height and weight. It is useful for context, but it does not describe body composition by itself.

    Daily calories

    This is an estimate based on your details and activity level. Your actual needs can vary, so use it as a starting point rather than an exact target.

    Water

    This gives a simple starting estimate based on body weight. Your needs can change with heat, exercise, diet and individual circumstances.

    Keto macros

    This provides a practical starting point for protein, net carbs and fat. Adjustments may be needed depending on your goals and individual needs.

    Tip: Use these results to understand your starting point and track your progress over time. They are estimates, not medical advice.
